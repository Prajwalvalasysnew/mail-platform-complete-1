# Regulated-workload policy and activation

The default platform posture prohibits message bodies, attachments, and
support tickets containing payment-card data, PHI, FERPA education records,
or broker-dealer records. Classification runs after template rendering and
before persistence. Findings contain category and field names only; matched
values are never logged.

Regulated handling is an Enterprise-only, per-tenant, per-framework mode:

| Mode | Delivery path | Rights/record workflow | External dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIPAA | encrypted secure-message portal | access, amendment, restriction, confidential communication, disclosure accounting, breach assessment | executed BAA chain and independent HIPAA security/privacy assessment |
| FERPA | encrypted secure-message portal | access, amendment, hearing, disclosure accounting and redisclosure restriction | direct-control terms and independent FERPA assessment |
| SEC/FINRA | ordinary delivery only after fail-closed archive acknowledgement | immutable specialist receipts and reconciliation queue | approved SEC 17a-4/FINRA archive and specialist assessment |

## Activation state machine

`disabled → pending → active → suspended` is enforced in the database-backed
service. Rejection returns a request to `rejected`; the tenant must request it
again. A tenant cannot activate itself.

Every named prerequisite requires a checksum-addressed artifact submitted by
the tenant and approved by a different administrator. Expired, rejected,
missing, or self-approved evidence does not satisfy a gate. Direct boolean
claims in an activation request are ignored. Activation and suspension are
written to the tamper-evident audit chain; suspension requires a reason.

Independent assessments and contractual documents are intentionally not
generated by this repository. Their actual signed artifacts must be uploaded
to the organization's controlled evidence store and reviewed. Until then the
corresponding mode remains pending.

## Fail-closed runtime controls

- PAN is blocked for every mode; no regulated activation authorizes cardholder
  data in email or support.
- PHI and education records are blocked unless the matching mode is active.
- PHI/FERPA content should be delivered through the secure portal; portal
  payloads are encrypted, expire, require two separately delivered
  credentials, can be revoked, and return `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- Every outbound message for an active SEC/FINRA tenant is acknowledged by the
  specialist archive before the send is persisted or queued. Missing
  configuration, timeout, malformed receipt, or provider failure returns 503
  and accepts zero messages.
- Suspending a mode immediately restores the default content block.
- Audit-chain records and signed heads are exported to an independently
  administered, KMS-encrypted S3 Object-Lock bucket; production refuses a
  null/local sink or an unconfigured audit KMS key.

Modes are approved individually. Evidence for one mode never activates or
satisfies another.
